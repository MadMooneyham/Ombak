# Ombak 1.0

A gamelan-inspired instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1, by Lunar Eclipse Forge. 1.0 is its first full release. You
lead the core melody on the white keys; the ensemble interlocks around it, the gongs mark the cycle, and every
note shimmers with *ombak*, the beating of two instruments tuned a little apart. It plays the traditional forms,
and it slows right down for meditation, to a core note every 20 seconds.

**Package:** `ombak-1.0.fwsc`, identity `FM-1_74010000`. Install it with the web installer in Chrome
(https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/install/). It carries the update loader: you can always leave Ombak
again, for another firmware or M-VAVE's own.

Manual: [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md).

## What it plays

- **Ten measured tunings** from real ensembles: gendèr wayang (Peliatan, Ubud), low gendèr (Tabanan), angklung
  (Sangsit, Mas), Kyai Kanyut Mesem slendro and pelog (Surakarta), semar pagulingan saih pitu (Tampak Gangsul,
  Ksatria) and gong (Peliatan), with the Balinese modes selisir, tembung, sunaren and baro and the Javanese
  pathet nem and barang.
- **Seven gamelan** to start from: GENDER WAYANG, SEMAR PEGULINGAN, ANGKLUNG, STILL (Ombak's own, for
  meditation), GONG GEDE, GILAK and LADRANG; and four slots of your own (MINE).
- **The parts:** the core (jublag and jegogan, or gendèr), the kotekan in four styles (circling, norot, nyog cag,
  empat), the reyong, the gendèr's ornaments, all turning toward the next note you lead.
- **The gong forms:** FREE (any cycle, 4 to 32 beats), and gilak, lancaran, ketawang and ladrang as the
  tradition places them.
- **The motion and the space:** the breath (beats that swell and slow into the gong), the cues (faster,
  slower, the ending, as the kendang would), the loop, the tides, the pan, and a long shimmering reverb, from
  Penumbra.
- **HOME is the Ongkara,** its light the ensemble's swell and the gong's flare, never the beat; or a bronze gong.

## For playing

- Every key and FX can be held to shape what it plays: each gong's level, tone, hum, ring and places; each
  part's density, register, turn and sparseness; bends on a held note; and the throws (shimmer, freeze, dip,
  hush, ombak).
- **SLEEP:** in 20 to 90 minutes, the ensemble plays its ending and falls still.
- **A/B** on SHAPE, **MINE** in the library.
- **MIDI:** notes, gongs, the pedal, HOME's knobs and the tempo as CCs, program changes; a DAW's clock leads the
  tempo and the cycle stays in phase with it; Ombak's own clock, Start and Stop out; the keys out as notes.
- **Economy under load:** when the FM-1 works hard, quiet tails end sooner and fewer voices sound at once,
  before anything is heard to struggle.

## Respect

Ombak is inspired by gamelan; it is not a gamelan, and no substitute for hearing, or learning, the real thing.
Its tunings, names and the outlines of its techniques are borrowed with gratitude to the musicians of Bali and
Java. Where Ombak makes its own choices (STILL, FREE, the kotekan styles' simplifications), the manual says so.
If you know the tradition and something is wrong, please tell us.
