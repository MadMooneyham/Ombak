# Ombak 1.2

A gamelan-inspired instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1, by Lunar Eclipse Forge. 1.2 adds the selonding, the sacred
iron gamelan, and gives the black keys to the instruments: every part can now come in and go out from a key.

**Package:** `ombak-1.2.fwsc`, identity `FM-1_74010200`. Install it with the web installer in Chrome
(https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/install/). It carries the update loader. **Coming from 1.1, 1.0 or the
betas:** everything you shaped, your loop, your MINE slots and your settings come over, as they were.

Play it in the browser: https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/emu/ · cheat sheet:
https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/cheatsheet/ · manual: [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md)

## New

**SELONDING**
- The sacred iron gamelan of the Bali Aga villages, in the seven-tone tuning McPhee measured at Tenganan
  Pegeringsingan. Every part plays on iron keys over a wooden trough, glassy and long; the gongs' part is played
  on its large keys, as the selonding does. No kajar, drums or reyong; stately, with a slow, small shimmer.
- GAMELAN's new **KEYS** row puts any gamelan on iron, or back on bronze.
- Ombak's selonding is a careful approximation, not a reproduction: its sound is modelled, not sampled, and the
  real ensembles take five-tone modes from their seven tones, where Ombak plays all seven.

**The black keys, re-laid**
- **Black 6–10**: the kotekan, the reyong, the ornaments, the drums (the kendang and the ceng-ceng together) and
  the suling, each in or out, lit while in. Hold one to shape it.
- **Black 11**: the ending. Its hold now also sets GLIDE, how fast the ensemble follows a new tempo.
- **The tempo is ALGO's alone** (on HOME). The FASTER and SLOWER keys are gone: ALGO already glides, and gives
  the same steps. For meditation, turn ALGO down where you used to hold SLOWER.
