# Ombak 0.1.6 (beta)

A gamelan-inspired instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1, by Lunar Eclipse Forge. Lead the core melody on the white
keys and the ensemble answers, in measured Balinese and Javanese tunings; play the traditional forms, or slow
it right down for meditation. Manual: [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md). What Ombak is: [RELEASE-0.1.5.md](RELEASE-0.1.5.md).

**Package:** `ombak-0.1.6.fwsc`, identity `FM-1_74000106`. Install it with the web installer in Chrome
(https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/install/). It carries the update loader. Coming from 0.1.5: everything you
shaped, your MINE slots and your settings come over.

## Fixed

- **FM-1s sold since autumn 2026** carry a different flash chip (UC25HQ80). Ombak took it for unknown and kept
  nothing it saved past a power-off. It now knows both chips.

GPL-3.0-only. Built on Penumbra, X0X (Charles Vestal) and Felucca (Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton Instruments); see
LICENSING.md.
