# Ombak 1.1

A gamelan-inspired instrument for the M-VAVE FM-1, by Lunar Eclipse Forge. 1.1 brings in the drums and the flute,
the angsel's breaks, and a new gamelan, and you can now play Ombak in the browser before you install it.

**Package:** `ombak-1.1.fwsc`, identity `FM-1_74010100`. Install it with the web installer in Chrome
(https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/install/). It carries the update loader. **Coming from 1.0 or the 0.1
betas:** everything you shaped, your loop, your MINE slots and your settings come over, as they were.

Play it in the browser: https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/emu/ · cheat sheet:
https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/cheatsheet/ · manual: [MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md)

## New

**The kendang and the ceng-ceng**
- Two drums, the low *wadon* and the high *lanang*, interlocking as the kotekan's two parts do: the wadon's open
  *dag* on the strong places, the lanang's *tut* between and its slapped *pak* answering.
- The ceng-ceng, small cymbals, divide the beat into four.
- Both thin out as the beat slows, so they never chatter over slow playing. A new SHAPE page, DRUMS: their
  levels, BUSY and SLAP. GAMELAN has a row for each.

**Angsel**
- **SEL on HOME** (or MIDI CC 25): the next beat breaks. The whole ensemble strikes together, accented, on the
  beat and again late in it, with the drums and cymbals, and leaves the rest of the beat silent. Then the music
  goes on.

**The suling**
- A bamboo flute holding the core melody in one long breath, as Balinese players do with circular breathing:
  it glides to each new note, its vibrato growing in after it arrives. A new SHAPE page, SULING: BREATH, VIBRATO,
  REGISTER, GLIDE and its level. It plays with SEMAR PEGULINGAN and STILL.

**GONG KEBYAR**
- The brilliant twentieth-century Balinese style, in Peliatan's five-tone pelog: everything in, norot gangsa, a
  fast ombak. The kendang and ceng-ceng now play with SEMAR PEGULINGAN, GONG GEDE and GILAK too.

**MIDI CC OUT** (SYSTEM)
- HOME's knobs, the tempo, an angsel and the gamelan go out as CCs and program changes as you change them, so a
  DAW can record your playing on the FM-1.

**In the browser**
- The emulator runs Ombak's own code, compiled for the browser, with the FM-1's panel to play on: mouse, touch or
  computer keyboard. It saves in the browser, and reads its screen aloud as the FM-1 does.
- The cheat sheet, on one printable page, with a PDF.

GPL-3.0-only. Built on Penumbra, X0X (Charles Vestal, whose emulator this is) and Felucca (Leo Kuroshita, Hügelton
Instruments); the Ongkara from Noto Sans Balinese (SIL OFL). See LICENSING.md.
