# Ombak

**A shimmering bronze ensemble for the M-VAVE FM-1**, by Lunar Eclipse Forge.

Switch it on and it plays: gongs mark the cycle, interlocking bells and drums weave around a melody, and every
note shimmers with *ombak*, the beating of two instruments tuned slightly apart. Touch a white key and the
ensemble follows your melody; rest, and it carries on by itself. Eleven measured tunings from Bali and Java,
from lively processionals to slow meditation, and a sound-module mode to play its instruments from a DAW.

Try it in the browser first: https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/emu/

Ombak is inspired by gamelan, not a gamelan. It owes everything to the living traditions of Bali and Java
and the communities who play them.

**[MANUAL.md](MANUAL.md)** is how to play it. See [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) for where it stands. To hear the sound,
open [prototype/index.html](prototype/index.html) in a browser and play it from the computer keyboard.

Ombak 1.3 installs from https://madmooneyham.github.io/Ombak/install/ (Chrome); [BUILDING.md](BUILDING.md) builds it. It runs on
[Penumbra](https://github.com/MadMooneyham/Penumbra)'s platform; [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) credits everyone
whose work it stands on.
