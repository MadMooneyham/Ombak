Playing

  • SEQ starts the cycle with the gong; again, it stops and rings out.
  • White keys lead: the core lands on the note at the next beat, the parts turn toward it.
  • ALGO on HOME: the tempo, the ensemble gliding to it · black 11: the ending, closing on the gong.
  • SEL on HOME: an angsel, the next beat breaks into accented strokes.
  • ARP: the gongs by the cycle or by your hand (black 1–5).
  • Black 6–10: kotekan, reyong, ornaments, drums, suling in or out.

The loop: the core, for you

RECrecord the core from the next cycle · again: cancel
PLAYhold the loop (you lead) · again: back
PLAY held, or REC + PLAYclear it

Slow and still

  • STILL: a note every ten seconds, the suling's long breath.
  • ALGO down: as far as a note every 20 s.
  • FX held: FREEZE the space, HUSH the ensemble away.
  • SLEEP (SYSTEM): the ending on its own, after 20–90 min.

The gamelan (PRESETS)

  • GENDER WAYANG · SEMAR PEGULINGAN · ANGKLUNG
  • STILL (Ombak's own) · GONG GEDE · GILAK
  • LADRANG (Java) · GONG KEBYAR · SELONDING (iron)
  • Forms: FREE, gilak, lancaran, ketawang, ladrang · kotekan: circling, norot, nyog cag, empat

Good to know

  • It saves itself once the sound rests; SAVE saves now.
  • MIDI (channel 1): notes from middle C, black keys the gongs, CC 20–25, clock both ways.
  • Eleven measured tunings; the names and sources are in the manual.
